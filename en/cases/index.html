Brief

Maternity communities default to high-volume social mechanics. The concept had to hold long-form editorial and peer conversation without the pull of an endless feed.

Approach
01 Stories arrive in small, complete sets — ending the page is a feature, not a failure.
02 Open comment threads replaced by small trimester rooms with a guide present.
03 Long-form journal pieces lead the experience, so membership is asked for after trust is earned.
Outcome

A complete concept — feed, community rooms, editorial studio and events — in one design language, built from twelve component families over six weeks and ready to extend into a product build.

Brief

A hardware launch page has to carry industrial design, spec detail and motion in one view, then repeat across twelve regional markets without a rebuild.

Approach
01 A metal material system with defined macro and micro states, so surfaces stay consistent at every crop.
02 Motion held in tokens — pulse, scan and hover are declared once and reused.
03 A localisation layer separating copy and telemetry overlays from layout.
Outcome

Seventy-four components and eighteen scenes covering twelve markets, with the design and engineering stack documented as one delivery blueprint.

Brief

High-value villa bookings rarely close in one session. Discovery, private enquiry and confirmation all needed to sit in a single flow.

Brief

Careers sites usually reduce to a job list. The brief was to make teams, mission and location the reason someone applies.

Approach

Brief

Northbridge publishes daily across AI, market structure and enterprise innovation. The hub had to absorb that volume without reading as a link dump.

Approach
01 A strict nine-card grid — every insight gets identical space, hierarchy comes from imagery and category.
02 Category labels lead each card so researchers scan by theme before headline.
03 Ink, paper and one accent bar; hover lift is the only motion on the page.
Outcome

A template that takes weekly publishing volume without redesign, now the pattern for the full insights programme, with one call to action placed after the reader has value.

Brief

Streaming homepages have to serve discovery and sign-up at once, and the two usually compete for the same screen.

Approach

Brief

Institutional finance sites bury conviction under boilerplate. The brief asked for the opposite: one confident statement, numbers stated plainly, leadership given a real voice.

Approach
01 An editorial type scale — display headlines against a quiet sans body for broadsheet rhythm.
02 One number at a time: the AUM figure stands alone with its as-of date, no dashboards or counters.
03 A leadership interview module in place of stock photography.
Outcome

A restrained editorial system that scales across What We Do, Strategies and Insights while staying unmistakably institutional — delivered in eight weeks as a landing concept, grid and UI kit.
