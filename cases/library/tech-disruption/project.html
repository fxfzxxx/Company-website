Case Study · Research Hub

Making dense research feel effortless

Northbridge Capital publishes daily across AI, market structure, and enterprise innovation. The brief: an insight hub that handles editorial volume without ever feeling like a link dump — modern finance tone, zero clutter.

View the live concept

Design decisions

Six choices that keep a high-volume research feed readable, scannable, and unmistakably premium.

Typography

Space Grotesk for headlines

A geometric grotesque gives the hub a technical edge while Manrope keeps body text calm and legible at density.

Layout

A strict nine-card grid

Every insight occupies identical real estate. Hierarchy comes from imagery and category labels, not card size games.

Color

Ink, paper, one accent

A near-black header, warm paper background, and a single accent bar — the restraint reads as institutional confidence.

Information design

Category before headline

Small-caps category labels lead every card so researchers can scan by theme before committing to a headline.

Motion

Hover lift, nothing else

Cards lift 4px on hover with a soft shadow. No parallax, no autoplay — motion is a cursor acknowledgment, not a show.

Conversion

One ask, placed last

The newsletter is the page's single call to action, positioned after the reader has already gotten value from the grid.

The outcome

A research hub template that absorbs weekly publishing volume without redesign — the grid, category system, and tone now extend across Northbridge's full insights program. Explore the live concept to see it handle nine simultaneous stories.
