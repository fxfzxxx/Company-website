Design case study

Softness as a design system.

Luna Bloom Society asked for a digital home that feels like a held breath: calm, luminous, and intimate. This page documents the brief, the visual language, and the interaction decisions behind the concept.

Visual language

Editorial warmth, engineered.

Every surface was tuned to lower the reader's pulse: generous whitespace, gradient orbs instead of hard borders, and photography treated like keepsakes rather than content.

  • Cormorant Garamond headlines for a hand-pressed, journal feel
  • Blush, cream, and dusk-lavender palette with soft radial glows
  • Story cards modeled on a slowed-down social feed
See the feed in action
Typography Serif voice, sans support

Cormorant Garamond for emotion, Manrope for wayfinding and UI labels.

Color Dawn palette

Blush #f5e3e0, cream #faf6f0, and lavender dusk accents with soft glows.

Motion Reveal, never bounce

Slow fade-and-rise reveals; counters ease in like a breath, not a scoreboard.

Components Ritual cards

Story, studio, and event cards share one rhythm so the feed reads as a whole.

Interaction decisions

Three choices that shaped the experience.

The concept lives or dies on restraint. These decisions kept the community feeling high-touch instead of high-volume.

A feed without infinite scroll

Stories arrive in small, complete sets. Ending the page is a feature — members leave calm, not drained.

Decision 01 Feed design

Rooms instead of comments

Conversation happens in small trimester circles with a guide present, replacing open comment threads entirely.

Decision 02 Community design

Editorial as the front door

Long-form journal pieces lead the experience, so the brand earns trust before asking for membership.

Decision 03 Content strategy

The outcome.

A complete concept — feed, community rooms, editorial studio, and events — expressed in one serene design language, ready to extend into a full product build.
