Designing institutional confidence

Atlas Capital needed a digital presence that carries the weight of a $1.3T asset manager: quiet authority, editorial clarity, and zero decoration for decoration's sake. This case study covers the concept, system, and craft decisions behind the landing experience.

Deliverables: Brand-led landing concept, editorial grid, UI kit

Timeline: 8 weeks

Team: Brand design, product design, motion

The brief

Institutional finance sites tend to bury conviction under boilerplate. The brief asked for the opposite: lead with a single confident statement, let numbers speak plainly, and give leadership a voice through media rather than stock photography.

Design decisions

Editorial type scale

Playfair Display headlines against a quiet sans-serif body create a broadsheet rhythm — authority without shouting.

One number at a time

The $1.3T AUM stat stands alone with its as-of date. No dashboards, no counters — a single audited fact.

Media over stock

A leadership interview module anchors the page, giving the firm a human voice and a reason to return.

Outcome

The concept demonstrates how a restrained editorial system scales across What We Do, Strategies, and Insights sections while staying unmistakably institutional. It now serves as the visual north star for the full Atlas Capital site build-out.
