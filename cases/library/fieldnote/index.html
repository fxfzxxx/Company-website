Insight brief · 16 October

Fix the first import, and onboarding fixes itself.

Across eighteen interviews, eleven people abandoned their first import and typed their data in by hand. The template that would have prevented it is buried two clicks deep.

  1. Show the template before the upload, not after the error.
  2. Accept the three date formats customers actually use.
  3. Keep a link from every imported row to its source file.
